# Perspectiv Design Partner Program

## What this is

Perspectiv is a network monitoring & operations platform built for SMB and mid-market IT teams who've outgrown free tools and resent paying SolarWinds prices. We're at v0.3.x, shipping fast, and looking for **3-5 design partners** to run Perspectiv alongside their current monitoring tool for 60 days and help us shape what becomes v1.0.

If you've ever:

- Rebooted a 167GB SolarWinds server because Orion ate all the RAM (real story)
- Sat in a procurement meeting picking which features your team gets to keep this year
- Watched a SolarWinds upgrade weekend turn into a Tuesday
- Paid SQL Server license money for a monitoring tool's database

…this offer is for you.

---

## What you get

- **6 months of Perspectiv Business tier free** (normally $1,299/mo · $7,800 value)
- **50% off Year 1** when you convert ($7,800/year, 50% of standard $15,588 annual)
- **Free SolarWinds migration assistance** ($7,500 value) — we port your device inventory, alert rules, and dashboards so you never lose visibility during the transition
- **Free Quick Start onboarding** ($999 value) — 1-hour walkthrough, agent rollout, dashboard buildout
- **Direct line to engineering** — Slack, email, or voice. Whatever you find, you tell us, we fix it. Most issues get a same-day or next-day fix.
- **Influence over the roadmap** — your real-world pain points become the v0.4 / v1.0 priorities
- **Pre-launch case study** — co-written with you, on your terms (no required public testimonial)

**Total value of the design partner package: ~$16,000+ in software + services for a 60-day pilot.**

## What we ask

- **Run Perspectiv alongside your current tool for 60 days** on at least one site / business unit. We're not asking you to migrate your production monitoring — we're asking you to validate ours against ground truth.
- **30 minutes a week**, give or take, for a Slack/email check-in or a 15-minute call when something interesting happens
- **Honest feedback** — what works, what's confusing, what's missing, what's broken. We want the unfiltered version. Founders learn nothing from polite feedback.
- **2 reference calls** during pre-launch (we'll prep questions; you decide what to share)

That's it. No marketing commitment, no required quote on our website, no exclusivity.

## What we're shipping right now

As of v0.3.3:

- **Network monitoring** — SNMP v1/v2c/v3, NetFlow, syslog, traps, topology auto-discovery via LLDP/CDP
- **Server / app monitoring** — Perspectiv agent for Windows / Linux / macOS, psutil-based, no inbound firewall holes
- **Configuration backup** — daily snapshots, diff history, auto-restore
- **Service checks** — TCP, HTTP/HTTPS, DNS, ICMP, traceroute (Netpath)
- **IPAM** — subnet utilization, conflict detection
- **Custom dashboards & reports** — drag-to-place widgets, scheduled email digests
- **Alerting** — email, webhooks, escalation paths, dedup, cooldowns, ack workflow
- **Built-in SSH client** to managed devices
- **Lifecycle / EOL tracking** — see end-of-life dates across your fleet
- **System backup & restore** — full Perspectiv config in one click

Everything bundled. No modules to license separately.

## Where we're going (v0.4 / v1.0)

- Code-signed Windows installer (DigiCert EV cert in procurement)
- Auto-discovery polish — protocol detection, agent vs SNMP recommendation
- Pricing page + public marketing site (you're holding the draft right now)
- Distributed pollers for multi-site customers
- Cloud-hosted SaaS option (alongside the self-hosted offering)
- Native multi-tenant for MSP partners

Your pain points are how we prioritize.

## Who's a fit

Best-fit design partners are:

- IT teams running **50-500 fully-monitored devices** across **1-3 sites**
- Currently on SolarWinds, LogicMonitor, Auvik, NinjaOne, or PRTG
- Have at least one IT person who'd write the install script themselves if they had to
- Use Linux, Docker, or both — Perspectiv is a Linux container, runs anywhere Docker runs

Not a fit:
- Enterprises with >5,000 nodes — talk to us in v1.0 when distributed pollers ship
- Teams with no Linux/Docker exposure — the install path assumes some comfort with `docker compose`
- Compliance-heavy regulated industries that require FedRAMP or equivalent today — we're not there yet

## How to apply

Send a one-paragraph email to **[partners@perspectiv.net](mailto:partners@perspectiv.net?subject=Design%20Partner%20Application)** with:

1. **Who you are** — name, role, company (or just "IT director, 200-person manufacturing co" if anonymity helps)
2. **What you currently use for monitoring** — and what specifically frustrates you about it
3. **Roughly how many devices you'd put under Perspectiv** during the 60-day pilot
4. **One thing you wish your current tool did** that it doesn't

We'll respond within 48 hours. If it's a fit, we'll schedule a 30-minute call to walk through the install path and pick a start date.

If you've been frustrated with monitoring tools for years, this is your chance to shape what fixes them.
